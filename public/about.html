About

The .exposed network

A set of independent sites that each track one subject from named public sources, and this hub that shows them together.

What it is

Each site researches and publishes documented issues in its subject, daily, with every record scored under a published rubric and tied to its sources. The hub reads one small file from each site and shows the headline fact, the sources and a shared map. The sites and the hub follow the same standards.

Sites

  • water.exposed: U.S. water issues, by state.
  • waste.exposed: U.S. waste, recycling and waste-to-energy, by state.
  • oceans.exposed: marine and coastal issues across 32 regional seas and the high seas.

How a site joins

  1. Follow the standards and publish a full data set as open JSON.
  2. Publish /network/stats.json and /network/layer.geojson under the contract.
  3. Carry the shared network bar and footer strip from network/chrome/latest/.
  4. Open a pull request adding the site to network/sites.json in the hub's repository.

Code and contact

The hub's code, the contract, the refresh job and this page's history are public at github.com/Carboman/earth-exposed. Questions, corrections and join requests go through that repository's issues.

What this is not

Not a census of every issue in any subject. Not a ranking of places or sites. Not advice. Each site states its coverage limits on its own pages.
