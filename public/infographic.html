    How to read this page

    • Every figure comes from the live feed and re-renders when a new run is published. Nothing is hand-typed.
    • Levels follow the 0–10 score: Critical 9–10, High 7–8, Moderate 4–6, Low 0–3. Rank is within a category only; categories are never ranked against each other.
    • Grey seas, empty categories and missing figures mean nothing was found in this feed, not that there is no problem.
