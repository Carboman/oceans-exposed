    Severity by category

    Every active finding, grouped by category and scored 0–10. Categories are ranked separately, never against each other.

    What drives each score

    The five scoring components, 0–2 each. The more strongly shaded the cell, the more severe the documented evidence.

    Primary issue layer

    What kind of problem each finding mainly is.

    Climate & Physical: warming, sea level, acidification, ice. Pollution: plastics, nutrients, chemicals and oil. Living Resources & Habitats: reefs, fisheries, habitats, wildlife, aquaculture. Governance & Economy: shipping, energy, mining, treaties, disputes. More

    Economic risk

    Risk to a fishery, a port, a coastal economy or an operator, from the cited evidence.

    Trend direction

    Observed direction for each active finding. Early runs have little comparable history, so many findings read as insufficient history.

    Ranked findings by category

    Ranked within each category by severity score, with the newer source date breaking ties. Categories are not combined into a global rank.

    Resolved or stabilized milestones

    Recorded for context. These are not ranked as current findings.
