Global Ocean Issues Tracker

Ranked, sourced findings on ocean warming, sea level, acidification, coral reefs, fisheries, plastics, pollution, shipping, offshore energy, deep-sea mining, coastal habitats, wildlife, polar seas, governance and aquaculture, across 32 regional seas and the high seas. Each finding is scored from 0 to 10 on documented evidence only.

    Where the findings are

    Each regional sea is shaded by its most severe active finding; glowing points are findings with coordinates. Hover for details, click a sea for its page.

    Drawing the map…

    Tiles sit on a fixed grid, grouped by ocean basin: Arctic at the top, Southern Ocean at the bottom, Pacific on the left, Atlantic in the centre, Indian on the right.

    Critical 9–10 High 7–8 Moderate 4–6 Low 0–3 No active finding in feed
    Basin (bottom edge): Pacific Atlantic Indian Southern Arctic

    Grey or untinted means no finding in this feed. It does not mean the sea has no problems. Sea outlines are approximate; findings spanning several seas shade every sea they touch. Open-ocean and global findings with no regional sea appear only in the lists and the Explorer.
